Institutional Background

What is Nenasala Institute?

'Nenasala' (Knowledge Centre) is a national telecentre network established under the Information and Communication Technology Agency (ICTA) of Sri Lanka to enhance digital literacy, IT skills, and modern computer knowledge among students and communities across the island.

Nenasala Institute of Computer Education Bolape (NICEB) operates as a premier educational technology hub, offering structured professional computer courses, G.C.E. Ordinary Level and Advanced Level ICT academic instruction, and state-of-the-art computer lab facilities.

Key Objectives

  • Empowering students with national school ICT curriculum excellence.
  • Hands-on practical computer programming and software proficiency.
  • Dedicated guidance for O/L and A/L university entrance examinations.
